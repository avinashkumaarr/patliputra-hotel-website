The Hotel

Welcome to Hotel Patliputra Continental

Strategically located at PC Golambar, Bypass Crossing, Anisabad, Hotel Patliputra Continental stands as one of Patna's distinguished 5-star hospitality destinations, situated just 5 minutes away from Jay Prakash Narayan International Airport (PAT).

Whether visiting for high-level business conventions, state galas, or cultural exploration of ancient Magadh, guests enjoy thoughtfully designed accommodations across 5 room categories, 3 specialty restaurants, 4 banquet halls, and dedicated wellness facilities.
